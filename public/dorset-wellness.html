Wellness

A hot tub on every deck, a shower under the oaks, a copper bath by the window and a swing for two. Each treehouse has its own, and each is a little different.

1 / 3

The Woodsman’s Treehouse

The open-air tree shower

On the lower deck, open to the wood, a shower is strapped to the trunk of the oak, with the same endless hot water as inside.

Guests swear by it in the rain, and in the snow.

1 / 2

The Woodsman’s Treehouse

The copper bath

A roll-top copper bath stands in the round central room, in front of a full-height window onto the trunk of the ancient oak the treehouse is built around.

Turn the wood burner to face it, and run it deep.

1 / 3

The Woodsman’s Treehouse

Rooftop sauna and hot tub

A spiral stair climbs to a deck in the canopy, with a sauna at one end and a ceramic hot tub at the other.

The sauna takes about fifteen minutes to heat. The tub is filled fresh with hot water just before you want it, so there is no fire to tend, and after dark there is nothing above you but oak and sky.

1 / 3

All three treehouses

Woodburner

All three treehouses have a woodburner, and the log basket is full when you arrive, with refills free.

In the Woodsman’s a cast-iron burner turns on its base, so the fire can face the bed, the bath or the kitchen. Dazzle’s ceramic stove stands between the sitting room and the bedroom and warms both at once, and Pinwheel’s terracotta stove warms the glass-roofed centre and all four rooms off it.

1 / 2

Dazzle Treehouse

The cargo-net day bed

Slung above the stream on the rear deck, the cargo net is a day bed for two, and an hour on it with a book is the thing guests write to us about most often.

It will be damp after rain, and it is for lying on, not standing.

1 / 2

Dazzle Treehouse

The tin hot tub over the water

On the rear deck, under the oaks, a circular tin hot tub fills with fresh hot water from its own taps whenever you want it, with a button for the bubbles.

The pizza oven and the fire pit are a few steps away.

Dazzle Treehouse

The glass spine

A spine of glass runs the length of the roof, filling Dazzle with leaf-light and keeping the canopy in view from every room.

There is a blind for it, best drawn last thing, once you are in bed.

1 / 2

Pinwheel Treehouse

A swing for two

Out on Pinwheel’s deck, in its ring of oaks, hangs a swing built for two people sitting face to face. It is stronger than it looks, and still not a climbing frame.

The deck faces the sunset, with a table, the pizza oven and a fire pit for the evening.

1 / 2

Pinwheel Treehouse

The square tin hot tub

Pinwheel’s square tin tub sits on the deck and is filled fresh with hot water whenever you want it.

Cover it between soaks, or the oaks will fill it with leaves.

Pinwheel Treehouse

Glass roof, four windows

Four rooms spin off a living space roofed in glass, and each ends in a full-height window facing a different point of the compass. The glass is one-way, so the wood can never look back in.

Blinds are fitted to all four windows, and the glass roof has its own.

Choose your treehouse

Every treehouse has its own hot tub, wood burner, pizza oven and fire pit. Nothing is shared.

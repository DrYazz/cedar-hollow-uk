Awards & recognition

We’re delighted that Cedar Hollow Oxford has been recognised nationally by VisitEngland, winning Silver for Best Self-Catering Accommodation of the Year 2026.

What began as a family dream to create a magical woodland retreat near Oxford has grown into something far beyond what we imagined - and we’re so grateful to every guest, volunteer, friend and supporter who has been part of the journey.

But Cedar Hollow has always been about more than just magical stays.

That is why we also support the Congenital Anaemia Network, helping people and families affected by inherited blood disorders.

Every stay helps us keep building something magical - and meaningful.

Cedar Hollow Oxford was formerly known as Cedar Hollow @ The Oaks. Same woodland, same family, same three retreats - now part of the Cedar Hollow collection alongside our Dorset woodland.

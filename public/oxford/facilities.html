Facilities

Tennis court, Swimming Pool, Jacuzzi, Table Tennis, Horse Riding and much more. You will certainly not be bored in Narnia!

See it on the site map

1 / 7

Tennis Court

Tennis rackets and tennis balls are available free. You just need to book your time on our webapp (for free) to coordinate with other guests.

Take a 3D tour of the tennis court

1 / 3

Honesty Bar with Outdoor Cinema

The bar can also be set up as an outdoor cinema. Book it in our webapp and we’ll bring you the projector. Priority is given to Beavers Den guests as all other rentals have their own projector and/or TV facilities.

Take a 3D tour of the honesty bar

1 / 4

Fire pit

Two fire pits are available for our guests to use and all firewood and firelighters are available free of charge.

Take a 3D tour of the fire pit

1 / 10

Trampoline, Slackline, Swings & Zipline

Trampoline, Swings & Slackline are available dotted around the estate and available for everyone to use.

The zipline however is exclusive to the treehouse guests as the zipline starts in the treehouse itself!

1 / 2

Muntjac Meadow

Nestled beside one of our orchards, Muntjac Meadow is a grassy haven where young (and not-so-young) guests can kick off a friendly match between two football goals. Prefer something a little more genteel? Rent a croquet set or a badminton kit, and we’ll happily transform the meadow into your own woodland court - perfect for gentle games and enchanted afternoons beneath the trees.

Take a 3D tour of the meadow

Wellness Facilities

Wellbeing at Cedar Hollow Oxford is about slowing down and finding peace in the simplest of pleasures.

Our wellness facilities include our woodland jacuzzi, our bubbling hot tub, the warmth of our sauna and our indoor swimming pool.

View our Wellness Facilities

1 / 8

The Stone Table

We have a communal dining table with Narnia inspired animal carvings all around it and a table made of Jurassic slate from Dorset. The table is near one of our woodland BBQs and is perfect for groups who’ve booked multiple rentals.

Take a 3D tour of the communal dining area

1 / 3

The Pavilion

Guarded by stone lions and tucked beneath the trees, The Pavilion is a quiet woodland spot for morning coffee, afternoon tea or a game of cards.

Take a 3D tour of the Pavilion garden

1 / 5

1962 Routemaster Bus

A classic red London Routemaster, hidden in the woods.

Downstairs is our intimate movie and magic room, with cinema seating, popcorn and live magic. Upstairs, the original top deck has become a cosy woodland café for coffee and drinks.

Part cinema, part café, part magic theatre. Missing your stop is encouraged.

1 / 3

Self Check-in

Guests check in easily using our private guest web-app.

From your phone you get an electronic key to your rental, access arrival instructions, book facilities, order breakfast and room service, and message our on-call team if you need anything during your stay.

Simple, contactless, and available throughout your visit.

    Around the estate

    A C.S. Lewis Inspired
    Fantasy Retreat

    Cedar Hollow Oxford is a collection of immersive stays inspired by Narnia, fantasy, and quiet enchantment.

    Each one is created to bring a touch of magic and create memorable stays in twelve acres of private woodland on the edge of Oxford.

    Read Our Story

    Together We Can

    We founded the Congenital Anaemia Network, a national charity for people living with rare inherited blood disorders. A portion of every stay helps sustain it. One weekend each month the retreats go to patients and their families, free of charge.

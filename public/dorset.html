Explore Dorset

Set within an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty where Dorset, Devon, and Somerset meet, with easy access to the Jurassic coast at Lyme Regis (10 miles) and Bridport (16 miles).

Nearby highlights include River Cottage, Forde Abbey, plus a strong selection of farm shops and well-regarded local dining.

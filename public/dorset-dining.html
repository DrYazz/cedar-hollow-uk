Eat in, or out

Dining In & Out

A wood-fired pizza oven on every deck, a full kitchen inside, and some of the best cooking in the West Country within forty minutes.

1 / 3

The pizza oven

Every treehouse has its own wood-fired pizza oven on its deck, with logs, kindling, firelighters and flour supplied.

Bring your own tomato and toppings, or book frozen pizza dough from the woodland shop when you book: order it up to the day before you arrive and it will be in your fridge, defrosting, on the morning you get here.

Allow forty-five minutes for the oven to come up to heat. Each pizza then takes a minute or three, and the first is always the worst one.

Cooking facilities

Each treehouse has a fully equipped kitchen: a hob, an electric oven, a sink, a fridge with an ice box, a kettle and a Nespresso machine, with the crockery, oil and flour you will need.

Outside, every deck has a barbecue and a fire pit, each on its own metal safety disc.

Private Chef Experiences

For special occasions, we highly recommend arranging a private chef in advance.

We can highly recommend Yhangry, who offer bespoke private dining experiences with a wide range of local chefs - from relaxed, affordable meals to luxurious menus created by Michelin-starred and MasterChef-winning chefs.

Woodland dinners and catered events

On evenings we announce ahead, we hold a woodland pop-up dinner: a table among the trees, with dinner cooked and served for you. Ask us when the next one is.

Book all three treehouses and the woodland is yours alone, and we can cater your own occasion, from a long dinner under the oaks to a whole celebration weekend.

Food shops and the woodland shop

There is no shop in the woods beyond the woodland shop, which runs on an honesty system: cider, fizz, frozen pizza dough, kindling and firelighters, whenever you want them. It will take an order up to the day before you arrive.

The nearest provisions are a few minutes up the road, so stock up on the way in if you can:

Thorncombe Village Shop, 1 mile: deli counter, local fruit, vegetables and meat, and a café. Monday and Tuesday 8.30–2, Wednesday to Friday 8.30–4, Saturday 8.30–1, Sunday 9.30–12.30.

Tytherleigh Farm Shop, 2 miles: local produce, fresh vegetables and the makings of dinner, with a food counter, The Cabin.

Morrisons Daily, South Chard, 3 miles: the one that is open when nothing else is, with a Post Office counter. Every day, 7am–9pm.

Millers Farm Shop, Kilmington, 7.5 miles: home-grown vegetables, meat from local farms and fish when it comes in, and the Teapot Café. Monday to Saturday 7.30–5.

Supermarket delivery

Sainsbury’s and Waitrose both deliver here, so the fridge can be full before you have unpacked. Book a slot for the afternoon you arrive, or for any day of your stay.

Give this as the delivery address: Cedar Hollow Dorset, Yonder Hill, Holditch, West Dorset TA20 4NL.

Delivery vans cannot come down into the woods, so you will need to meet the driver in the car park at the top, five minutes’ walk up from the treehouses. Sat-navs often send drivers into Holditch village instead, so put the car park’s what3words, ///emerald.newlywed.gathers, and your mobile number in the delivery notes, and keep your phone close around the slot time: the signal comes and goes in the lanes.

Where to eat around us

West Dorset, East Devon and South Somerset meet around the woods, and the cooking within forty minutes is as good as anywhere in the West Country. None of these holds a Michelin star, but most are in the Michelin Guide, so book the busiest well ahead. The nearest star is Lympstone Manor, near Exmouth, about an hour away.

  • The Tytherleigh Arms

    Tytherleigh · 2 miles, 8 min

    A sixteenth-century coaching inn with rooms, cooking modern British food from West Country produce. Our local, and our favourite.

  • The Cotley Inn

    Wambrook · 7 miles, 20 min

    A country pub with rooms in a seventeenth-century farmhouse: seasonal, nose-to-tail cooking from animals reared on site and its own kitchen garden.

  • Brassica

    Lyme Regis · 10.5 miles, 25 min

    Photo: Des Blenkinsopp, CC BY-SA 2.0

More relaxed, and also good

Cafés, pubs and the places we send people who want a good lunch rather than an occasion.

    Hugh Fearnley-Whittingstall’s kitchen and store on the farm: breakfast, brunch and lunch.

    Thursday to Monday, 9–4

  • Rockfish

    Lyme Regis · 10.5 miles, 25 minutes

    Locally landed fish and seafood on Cobb Road, looking over the harbour.

    Lyme Regis · 10.5 miles, 25 minutes

    A café, bar and restaurant right on the beachfront: burgers, fish and a very large breakfast.

    Every day, 9am–10pm

  • The Anchor Inn

    Seatown · 15 miles, 30 minutes

    On the beach below Golden Cap, full of coast-path walkers and cold-water swimmers in roughly equal measure.

    Kitchen 12–8 every day

  • The Bull Hotel

    Bridport · 14 miles, 33 minutes

    Boutique rooms upstairs from a good brunch, moules-frites and a courtyard worth the walk.

  • Sladers Yard

    West Bay · 19 miles, 36 minutes

    Wednesday to Saturday, 10–4; Saturday suppers

    Crewkerne · 10.5 miles, 22 minutes

    Fresh, local and organic, in a town most people drive through without stopping.

  • Crab House Café

    Wyke Regis · 35 miles, 67 minutes

    A long way, and worth it: oysters from its own beds and crab served with hammers, buckets and bibs.

    Wednesday to Sunday; book
